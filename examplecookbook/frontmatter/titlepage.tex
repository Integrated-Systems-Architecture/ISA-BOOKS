This booklet is one of four companions: Design, Verification, Simulation,
and this worked Example. A CORDIC rotator, small enough to read in one
sitting, is developed in VHDL and in SystemVerilog and verified with six
testbenches in three languages. Every source file is reproduced in full and
distributed alongside this document under \file{code/cordic/}; every one of
them has been analysed and simulated before being typeset.
