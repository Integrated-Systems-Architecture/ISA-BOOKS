\small\itshape
This booklet is a companion reference, not a build-systems course. It covers
exactly enough of \FuseSoC, vendoring, \tool{make} and the register\_interface
regtool to read the \file{.core} files, Makefiles and \file{.hjson} register
descriptions that ship with the lab folders, and to write new ones of your
own. For anything beyond this scope, the official \FuseSoC documentation at
\texttt{fusesoc.readthedocs.io}, GNU \tool{make}'s manual, and the OpenTitan
regtool documentation are the authoritative sources.
\end{minipage}
